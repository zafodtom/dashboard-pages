Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Home Dashboard is a personal, non-commercial dashboard operated by its owner. This policy describes the intended handling of Google Calendar data by the application.

Data accessed

With the account holder's permission, Home Dashboard reads Google Calendar event information, including event titles, dates and times, for display on the owner's private dashboard.

Purpose and use

Calendar information is used to display upcoming events. Home Dashboard does not use Google Calendar data for advertising, profiling or selling information.

Storage and security

Calendar access credentials are held in the owner's private server configuration. Calendar data may be temporarily cached on the owner's server to operate the dashboard. The public website hosting this policy does not receive Google Calendar credentials or event data.

Sharing

Google Calendar data is not intentionally shared with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Google provides access to the data through its APIs under the account holder's authorization.

Control

The account holder can revoke access at any time in Google Account connections. Local cached data and stored credentials can be removed by the dashboard operator.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the contact address shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
